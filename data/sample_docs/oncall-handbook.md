# On-Call Handbook

## Rotations

Each product team runs a weekly on-call rotation that starts Monday at 10:00 local time. Team Atlas runs a follow-the-sun rotation between the New York and Bangalore offices. Rotations are scheduled in PagerDuty, which integrates with Slack and Datadog.

## Expectations

The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge pages within 5 minutes during their shift. A secondary on-call engineer is paged automatically if the primary does not acknowledge within 10 minutes. On-call engineers should keep a laptop and a stable internet connection available at all times during their shift.

## Compensation

Engineers receive one day of time off in lieu for every weekend on-call shift. Time off in lieu must be used within 60 days and is requested through Workday.

## Handoff

At the end of each rotation the outgoing engineer writes a handoff note in the team on-call channel that lists open incidents, noisy alerts, and pending follow-ups. Noisy alerts that page more than three times per week without action must be tuned or deleted.
